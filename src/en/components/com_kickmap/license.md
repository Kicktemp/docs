## What the purchase includes

The price on [kicktemp.shop](https://kicktemp.shop/en/products/kick-map) does not pay for a usage right, but for the
service around KickMap. Each plan runs for one year:

| Plan | Websites | Included for one year |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | 1–3 | Downloads, updates via the Joomla updater, support |
| Business | 25 | Downloads, updates via the Joomla updater, support |
| Agency | 99 | Downloads, updates via the Joomla updater, support |

The number of websites refers to the domains that can be registered for updates
(see [Updates & Licence](./updates)). After the year ends, KickMap keeps working without restrictions. Only new
downloads, updates and support require a renewal.

The licence limits neither the number of locations nor the number of maps.
